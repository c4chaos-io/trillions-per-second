# EPIC 💎 // THE SIDE QUESTS

### On Superintelligence, Simulation, and Waking Up

**EPIC** is the anthology series housed in this folder. EPIC stands for *Every Piece Is Connected*.

**Trillions Per Second (TPS)** is the main quest: the book this repository holds. These are its side quests. Each piece is a standalone paper or essay that opens a different door, and every door routes the reader back to the main quest:

> Point the oldest maps of the mind at the newest minds on earth. Keep what a skeptic can check. Route everything back to the main quest: hack the simulation, wake up.

The pieces are a stack, not a pile. Each one carries forward what the earlier ones established, and the cross-references between them are the point: the connections are shown, not just claimed.

## THE HOUSE RULES

* **Open Heart, Open Mind, Open Source.** The work ships open because it serves the Dharma, the original open source. Papers carry a CC BY 4.0 license note; see the repo [LICENSE](../LICENSE) for the book.
* **Centaur, stated plainly.** Drafted with an AI, red-teamed by frontier models, byline on the cover. The human brings the taste, the thesis, and the veto.
* **No home-team exemptions.** Where the old maps break, the anthology says so.
* **Outsider honesty.** No lab, no credentials, no pretense. Conceptual reframing and pilot-scale work, pointing at the experiments others should run.
* **Bait with honesty.** The metaphysics is the bait. Waking up is the payload, and the anthology says so up front.
